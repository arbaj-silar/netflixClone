About Money Heist

Money Heist, originally titled La Casa de Papel, became one of Netflix’s biggest global sensations. The story follows a mysterious criminal strategist, known only as The Professor, who plans two massive heists — one inside the Royal Mint of Spain and another inside the Bank of Spain. Unlike traditional crime dramas, the series focuses deeply on emotions, human psychology, loyalty, and the consequences of greed and power.

Every member of the gang adopts a city codename such as Tokyo, Berlin, Nairobi, Rio, Denver, Helsinki, and Moscow. As the operation unfolds, relationships become complicated, tensions rise, and the crew faces impossible choices.
